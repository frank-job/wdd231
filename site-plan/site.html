Site Name

Econ tourism site "Jinja Tourism Hub"

Reason: The name "Jinja Tourism Hub" clearly identifies the site's focus on tourism in Jinja, a major tourist destination in Uganda. It is short, memorable, and directly communicates the site's purpose.

Site Purpose

This site helps tourists find the best hotels, boat rides, and other tourism-related services in Jinja, Uganda.

Scenarios

Questions my users will ask:

  • Where can I find a cheap hotel in Jinja?
  • How much does rafting cost?

Color Scheme

I will use these colors to represent [e.g., The water of the Nile and the green nature]:

Primary: #004d40
Secondary: #ffca28
Accent: #f5f5f5
Text: #333333

Typography

Heading Font: Roboto Mono - This is how the headings will look. Bold and modern.
Body Font: Open Sans - This is how the paragraphs will look. Readable and clean.
